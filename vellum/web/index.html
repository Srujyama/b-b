How it works

Vellum reads a hospital bill line by line and finds the billing errors. It then negotiates the bill down with the hospital, never above the limit the patient sets. The patient's money waits in escrow and is paid only when the hospital confirms the account is at $0.

  1. Mrs. Lin's billClaude reads every line and marks the likely errors.
  2. Her informationHousehold, income, the most she can pay, and her consent.
  3. ResearchMedicare rates and the hospital's own charity policy, with sources.
  4. The callVellum's advocate talks the bill down with the hospital's billing rep.
  5. SummaryWhat was billed, removed and agreed, and what happens next.
